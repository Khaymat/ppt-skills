# Illustration prompts

Base prompt (works with most image generators):

> pen and ink etching illustration with soft grey watercolor wash, New Yorker magazine style, monochrome,
> off-white paper background, no color, no text, [SUBJECT]

Replace `[SUBJECT]` per slide. Ask for a wide 2:1 image for covers and a 4:3 image for framed panels.

| Slide purpose | Subject idea |
|---|---|
| Cover or city or institution | a lone figure walking past a row of tall buildings beside a canal |
| Crisis, decline, risk | a paper ticker tape curling off a desk with a falling zigzag line and a coffee cup |
| Trade-offs, cost, balance | a brass balance scale with coins on one pan and papers on the other |
| Theory, formulas | an open notebook full of handwritten equations with a fountain pen and a cup |
| Perspective, outlook, regimes | a round barometer on a wall beside a window with a stormy sky |
| Future, ending | a tree-lined wet street with lamp posts, a figure walking away into mist |
| Method, plan, steps | a hand-drawn map with a compass rose and dotted route |
| Time, schedule | a wall calendar and an hourglass on a wooden desk |

## Checks before using an image

- No colour cast: convert to greyscale and tint to the paper tone if the generator adds colour.
- No text or logos inside the picture; put words in captions.
- Resolution of at least 1440 px wide for panels and 1920 px wide for covers.
- Keep generator terms of use and licences in mind before publishing a deck publicly.

Until real images exist, use `"placeholder:skyline"` or `"placeholder:hills"` in the spec.
